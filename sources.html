The texts of ancient and classical Sanskrit works are the common heritage of all. The indexes on this site — the alphabetical pāda and sūtra indexes, references, metre identification, cross-references, restored sandhi and the arrangement by work, book and chapter — are prepared by Sanskrit Manthan for free, non-commercial study. The digital texts on which they are built were typed and edited by the scholars and institutions named below, from the printed editions named below. We gratefully acknowledge their work.

GRETIL (Göttingen Register of Electronic Texts in Indian Languages, gretil.sub.uni-goettingen.de): each GRETIL file states that it is for reference purposes and that copyright and terms of use are as for its source edition. The edition and the person who typed each text are listed below, as given in the GRETIL file. The Mahābhārata electronic text is © Bhandarkar Oriental Research Institute, Pune (1999), on the basis of the text entered by Muneo Tokunaga et al., revised by John Smith.

Dictionaries and grammar data.     The first and the second halves of a stanza for mathematical rules are sometimes interrupted by stanzas for mathematical examples. In both ApaTe and zarma the two halves are given different numbers, and the same numbering-system is followed in this digitalized version also.
    The numbers given to the stanzas by Colebrooke in his English translation is different from ours. They are noted as [Cb-].
