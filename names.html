नामानि · Names

Find the meaning of a Sanskrit name, or choose a Sanskrit name for a baby — by first letter, by birth star (nakṣatra-pāda syllable), or from the date and time of birth.

The birth star is calculated from the Moon's sidereal position (Lahiri ayanāṃśa), accurate to a few minutes. Name-syllable tables differ a little between regions — please confirm with your family priest or pañcāṅga.
