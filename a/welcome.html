स्वागतम् — Welcome to Sanskrit Manthan

2026-10-31 · संस्कृतमन्थनम् · Sanskrit Manthan

यथा पिण्डे तथा ब्रह्माण्डे — as in the small, so in the vast.

Sanskrit Manthan (संस्कृतमन्थनम्) is a bhāratīya-jñāna-paramparā-kāryaśālā — a workshop of Indian Knowledge Systems. Its aim is simple: to let anyone read the sources in the original, understand them, and live with them today.

What you will find here

  • Books — more than 150 works in Sanskrit and Telugu: the Purāṇas, Rāmāyaṇa and Mahābhārata in singable verse, kāvyas on the śāstras, Pāṇinian grammar and the Nāṭyaśāstra, kośas, stotras, education and vacana-kāvyas.
  • Search the sources — every pāda of classical kāvya, sūtra and kārikā texts, the Vedas and Upaniṣads, subhāṣitas, the Itihāsa–Purāṇa and the śāstras, in Devanāgarī, Telugu or Roman.
  • Kośa and grammar — dictionaries, every noun and verb form with its Pāṇinian derivation, lessons and games.
  • Greetings — original Sanskrit ślokas for festivals and occasions, with meanings in the languages of India, made into a card with your own photo.

Meet Bodhinī

Tap the lady in the saree at the bottom of any page. Bodhinī — “she who awakens understanding” — answers in Sanskrit, Telugu, Hindi or English: she finds a verse from its first words, opens Gītā 2.47 or Pāṇini 1.1.1, shows a book, gives a word's meaning, explains a grammar lesson, reads ślokas aloud, and takes you to any page — by typing or by voice.

Keep it on your phone

Add Sanskrit Manthan to your home screen (Bodhinī ▸ “Install app”) and save the indexes for offline reading.
