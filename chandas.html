छन्दोऽन्वेषणम्

Metre finder · type or paste a verse in Devanagari, Telugu or Roman (IAST) — its syllables are weighed, its gaṇas named and its metre identified with the lakṣaṇa

Examples:

Put each pāda on its own line, or each half-verse; a whole verse on one line also works. Daṇḍas, verse numbers and punctuation are ignored.

सर्वाणि छन्दांसि · all metres the finder knows
Syllable weight: a syllable is guru (–) when its vowel is long, when it carries anusvāra or visarga, or when a conjunct follows; otherwise laghu (⏑). The last syllable of a pāda may be either (pādānta-guru). Telugu padya rules of yati and prāsa are not checked. · श्लोकान्वेषणम् · sanskritmanthan.in
