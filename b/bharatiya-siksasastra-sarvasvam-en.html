A Compendium of Education in Sanskrit Sources · in Ten Volumes

English · English · Bhāratīya Śikṣāśāstra-Sarvasvam · 685 pp. · Dr. Mantha Srinivasu
Google PlayLibrary

Indian thought on education is scattered across the Vedas and Upaniṣads, the Dharmaśāstras, Āyurveda, the Arthaśāstra, the philosophical systems, kāvya and drama, Buddhist and Jaina texts, inscriptions and the literature of India's regional languages. This compendium gathers it, concept by concept, into one reference work: ten volumes in one book, 118 chapters and 584 units.

Every unit gives the original Sanskrit text (in IAST), its exact locus and a clear explanation. Every chapter ends with points for discussion, a comparison with modern theory (Piaget, Bloom, Dewey, Vygotsky, Erikson, Kohlberg, Shulman and others), links to B.Ed., ITEP, M.Ed., Śikṣāśāstrī and Śikṣācārya papers, a practicum activity, and recommended editions and translations.

The ten volumes: 1 Foundations — terminology, sources, historiography · 2 Philosophy of Education · 3 Learning — cognition, memory, attention · 4 Development and Individual Difference · 5 Sociology of Education · 6 Curriculum · 7 Methods of Teaching · 8 Teacher and Learner — the teacher-education core · 9 Assessment, Institutions and Administration · 10 Special Areas, History and Comparative Education.

Seven indexes make the work easy to use: first lines of quoted mantras and ślokas, texts cited with loci, persons, subjects, modern terms, a syllabus concordance, and a bibliography.

Sensitive questions — access and varṇa, women's education, punishment, disability — are presented honestly, with the restrictive passages and the counter-voices of the tradition side by side, and read against present-day law and policy.

Written for teacher educators, B.Ed. and M.Ed. students, researchers, and everyone integrating Indian Knowledge Systems into the curriculum under NEP 2020. Translated from the Sanskrit master edition; Sanskrit, Hindi and Telugu editions are also available, with identical unit numbers.
